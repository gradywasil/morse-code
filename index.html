CAUTION — FLASHING LIGHT

DO NOT WATCH IF FLASHING LIGHT HAS EVER TRIGGERED A SEIZURE.

This instrument speaks by flashing light. Anyone photosensitive should not watch the lamp or the screen.

  • Speed steps: LEARNER 5, STANDARD 8, BRISK 12 words per minute.
  • Repeated dots flash 2.1, 3.3 and 5.0 times per second across those steps.
  • The safety line is 3 flashes per second.
  • The default LEARNER step stays below the line.
